How to read these assessments

The frozen policy treats separately grounded observational origins as distinct evidence when coverage is declared complete. This is graph independence under explicit assumptions, not statistical independence or a claim of cultural truth. Support and conflict are separate evidence indicators. Missing ancestry can leave independence undetermined even when visible sources support the claim.

Solid arrows show recorded derivation or support; red arrows show challenges; dashed arrows show shared processing. A derivation arrow points from an artifact to its source. A support arrow points toward the supported claim. Select a node to inspect exact relation names.

The fixture inspector embeds synthetic data and is not an access-control system. Public-purpose assessment filters records in the Python backend; client-side hiding does not provide secrecy.
